# Stock Ninja brand vectors

Stock discs and confirmed bag badges use twenty scalable company logos rather than
issuer-hosted generic Robinhood thumbnails or approximate hand-drawn marks.
The SVGs retain their source geometry and original color. No CSS recoloring is
applied. A neutral pale logo chamber keeps genuine black marks readable without
tinting the artwork. Tickers remain visible underneath. Token addresses, pools and the
curated stock list are unchanged.

Retrieved 2026-10-07. Simple Icons sources are pinned to revision `98820a4`
(CC0 project; brand source/guideline metadata is in its catalog). Wikimedia files
are identified as public-domain geometric/text logos on their provenance pages.
Brand names and logos identify the corresponding assets and do not imply endorsement.

Strategy uses its 2025 identity. Micron uses its 2024 identity. Hims uses its
2026 wordmark. Vector wordmarks keep their original proportions inside the disc.

Updated 2026-10-07: Meta retains its blue gradient, NVIDIA its green/black
identity, Circle its official purple gradient favicon (256-unit SVG), Coinbase
its blue wordmark, Reddit its orange 2023 wordmark, Intel its two-tone 2020
wordmark, Tesla its red original, and Google its 2025 multicolor gradient G
(the Commons vector transcription of Google's artwork, not a manually recolored
monochrome G). Apple, SpaceX, AMD and Palantir legitimately use black marks;
they are not given invented colors. The original eight previously sourced
company vectors remain full color where their source artwork is colored.

The Tesla Motors source is geometric/text public-domain artwork. Google’s
Commons vector transcription is CC0. Brand identification remains subject to
its source trademark notices; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

NVIDIA displays the original green eye symbol via a cropped SVG viewBox; its
source path coordinates and colors are unchanged. This avoids squeezing its
long horizontal wordmark into a small coin.
